What DayTally does

Split a meal bill from a receipt photo. The app reads the prices. You check the names, then confirm.

  1. Receipt. Add a photo. DayTally reads the dishes, GST, and service charge.
  2. Who had what. Type each person and the dishes they had, or say them. More than one dish can go in the same box.
  3. Review. Fix names, choose who paid first, and add PayNow, bank, or e-money so friends know how to repay.
  4. Done. Each card shows one share. Press Mark as paid back when that person has sent the money. Copy or share the summary.
  5. History and Calendar. Past splits stay on this device. A confirmed split is linked to an event.
  6. Chatbot. Ask what you owe, who still owes you, the bill total, GST, or what someone ordered. Answers come from saved splits.
  7. Light and dark. Use the sun or moon beside Profile.
  8. Feedback. After a split, one short form per person. It does not change the bill.
